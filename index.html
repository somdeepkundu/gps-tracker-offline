📍 Privacy & GPS Consent

Your Privacy is Protected

✓ One-Way GPS Service

GPS data is collected only on YOUR device using the Geolocation API.

✓ NO Data Sharing

No data is sent to any server, cloud, or shared with anyone including the developer.

✓ Local Storage Only

All tracking data stays in your browser's IndexedDB. You control when to export or delete it.

✓ Fully Offline

This app works completely offline. No internet required to track your location.

✓ You're in Control

You can clear history, export data, or stop tracking anytime.

Browser Permission: Your browser will ask for location permission. You can deny or revoke it anytime in browser settings.
